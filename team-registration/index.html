ABOUT THE INSTITUTION

Innovation through experience

01

Acharya

Acharya is a premier educational community based in Bangalore, known for innovation, excellence and holistic student development. With a legacy of more than three decades, Acharya offers a diverse learning environment that nurtures talent and future leaders.

02

Institute of Technology

Acharya Institute of Technology (AIT), established in 2000 in Bengaluru, is an engineering and technology institution committed to teaching, research and professional development. It is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University and accredited by NAAC and NBA.

03

Mechatronics Engineering

Mechatronics engineering combines mechanical engineering, electronics, computer science and control engineering to prepare students for designing and implementing intelligent systems and products. The department's collaboration with Siemens supports exposure to current industry trends.
